Identify ionic salts in seconds with minimal sample preparation. The portable IonicX XRF analyzer offers fast, accurate raw material verification to pharmaceutical and biotech manufacturers, replacing costly and time-consuming chemical testing with a non-destructive, on-site method.

TruScan G3 Handheld Raman Analyzer

TruScan G3 advances the capabilities of raw material testing for pharmaceutical and biotechnology manufacturing, where speed and efficiency are key. Instead of expensive and time-consuming laboratory testing, TruScan G3 enables rapid identification of materials on-site – in the warehouse or at the receiving end. TruScan RM Handheld Raman Analyzer

In the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries, the quality of materials must be ensured from raw materials to finished products. The Thermo Scientific TruScan RM handheld analyzer enables reliable material verification through sealed packaging in seconds, right where it is needed.

With the optional TruTools package, users can create advanced methods for qualitative and quantitative analysis of more complex materials.

TruScan RM uses advanced optics and multivariate residue analysis to efficiently identify materials. The measurement principle is non-destructive and quickly verifies a wide range of chemicals, including cellulose-based products.

With TruTools, TruScan RM becomes an indispensable production tool. It can be used for quality control of raw materials, identification of ingredients and finished products, as well as monitoring the progress of reactions or mixing powders in real time.

Main features:

  • Weight less than 1 kg
  • Chemical and drop resistant
  • Analysis through plastic bags, glass containers, blisters and capsules
  • Adapted to the work environment with intuitive controls and the option of PDF reports
  • Suitable for detecting counterfeit medicines
  • Documentation and operating procedures are available for instrument qualification
